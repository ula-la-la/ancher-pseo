Critical synthesis

What TimeXer adds. A more explicit contract between the forecast target and outside signals.

Why finance is harder. Exogenous variables arrive at different frequencies, contain revisions, and can leak future information.

What is not yet proven here. Robust out-of-sample economic value after costs, across regimes and assets.
